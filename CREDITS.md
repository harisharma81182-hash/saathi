# Photo credits

Saathi's screens use real space photos. Each one was downloaded from its original source (no
copies from other sites), turned into small WebP files by `client/scripts/make-backgrounds.mjs`,
and lives in `client/public/bg/` as `<name>-desktop.webp` (1920 px) and `<name>-phone.webp`
(900 x 1500). The same list is shown in the app under Settings.

Licences:
- ESA/Hubble and ESA images: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://esahubble.org/copyright/), free to use with the credit shown.
- NASA images: public domain, not copyrighted ([NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)); credit "NASA".
- Pexels photos: the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/), free to use, credit given to the photographer anyway.

"Exact" means the same photo as Himanshu's reference screenshot. "Similar" means the exact original
could not be found, so the closest free photo (same subject, colours and mood) was used instead.
Paid or watermarked stock is never used. The HD originals are kept in `design/bg-hd/` (not in
Git, too large); the app only loads the small WebP files.
